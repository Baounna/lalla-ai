## Long version

A few months ago I joined a "Code for Health" hackathon. The project I built stuck with me — so I spent the last weeks turning it into something real.

Meet **Lalla AI**: an AI companion for breast-cancer awareness and self-examination.

**The problem:** breast cancer is the most common cancer in women worldwide, and survival exceeds 90% when caught early. Yet the guidance that saves lives is often locked behind clinical language and static diagrams that no one actually follows.

**What I built instead:**

🧊 **An interactive 3D self-exam guide.** Not images — a real-time 3D visualization (Three.js / React Three Fiber) you can rotate and zoom. As you advance through the 7 steps, the relevant region glows and a guide marker traces the correct circular + clock-method palpation path. It feels like a medical instrument, not a leaflet.

🤖 **A warm AI companion.** Ask anything about breast health and get a clear, kind answer — in your own words. The model is explicitly instructed to educate, never diagnose, and always recommend seeing a doctor for any concern.

🧮 **A private risk check + monthly reminder.** 7 questions, an AI-assisted summary, and a calendar reminder so the habit sticks. Zero data stored. No sign-up. No phone number.

🌍 **Multilingual & global.** It began for women in Morocco and now speaks English and French, designed for women everywhere.

**The stack:** Next.js 16 · React 19 · React Three Fiber (Three.js) · Tailwind v4 · Claude / Gemini APIs · deployed on Vercel.

The hardest and most rewarding part was the 3D guide — making something anatomically clear, tasteful, and genuinely useful in the browser, on a phone.

If this helps even one person do a self-check this month, it was worth it. 💖
