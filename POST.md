## 📖 LONG VERSION (story style)

A while ago I joined a "Code for Health" hackathon. The idea stuck with me, so I kept building — and turned it into something I'm genuinely proud of.

Meet **Lalla AI**: an AI companion for breast-cancer awareness and early detection.

The problem: breast cancer is the most common cancer in women (about 1 in 8 in their lifetime), and survival is over 90% when caught early. Yet the guidance that saves lives is usually locked behind clinical PDFs nobody actually follows.

So I built tools that *guide*, never diagnose:

🧊 **Interactive 3D self-exam** — a real-time model (Three.js) that lights up each region as you move through the steps.
🩻 **Mammogram density simulator** — toggle BI-RADS A→D and watch a finding get hidden by dense tissue. It explains *why* screening matters.
📷 **Camera visual check** — uses your camera as a guided mirror, 100% on your device, nothing uploaded.
💬 **AI companion** — answers questions in plain language, always points to a doctor.
🧮 Plus risk check, 12 signs, types & stages, glossary, "just diagnosed?" support, worldwide helplines, and clinician decision-support tools.

And because I wanted to prove the ML too: I trained a real classifier on the Wisconsin Breast Cancer dataset — 98.6% accuracy, 0.998 ROC-AUC, with explainability — shipped as a clearly-labeled **research demo, not a clinical device**.

🛠️ Stack: Next.js 16 · React 19 · React Three Fiber / Three.js · Claude API · Tailwind · Vercel. Bilingual (EN/FR), private, free.

The hardest part was staying honest: a phone can't detect cancer, so I never pretended it could. Everything is educational and points to real care — that's what makes it trustworthy.
